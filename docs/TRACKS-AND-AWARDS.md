# Badger BuildFest 2026: Tracks and Awards

This is a reference copy of the event information supplied by the team on 2026-09-26. Select exactly one track and, optionally, up to two challenges that the project meaningfully addresses. For this project, **Applied AI & Automation** is the selected track. **Open Venture** and **The Art of the Break** are the team's current, tentative challenge choices; no entry has been submitted.

## Tracks

Track finalists are judged live on Sunday.

| Track | Sponsor | Focus | First place | Second place |
|-------|---------|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Applied AI & Automation | Google | AI agents, SaaS, automation, productivity, business workflows, fintech, analytics, and developer tools | $1,500 | $1,000 |
| Health, Sustainability & Society | Tech Exploration Lab | Health, sustainability, education, accessibility, campus and community needs, and social-impact applications | $1,500 | $1,000 |
| Emerging Technology & Entrepreneurship | Wisconsin School of Business | Hardware, robotics, IoT, XR, blockchain, new products, ventures, and experimental technologies | $1,500 | $1,000 |

## Optional challenges

Teams may enter up to two challenges in addition to their track. Challenge eligibility is independent of track-finalist status. Challenges are judged asynchronously from submitted materials; a live challenge demo is not required.

### Owner-Side Communication & Decision Support: The Cost-of-Care Conversation

- **Sponsor:** School of Veterinary Medicine
- **Awards:** First place $500; second place $250
- **Prompt:** More than half of U.S. pet owners skip or decline vet-recommended care, often over cost, and many leave with a bill without understanding why each item matters or whether a lower-cost option exists. Turn that conversation into an informed, shared decision between owner and veterinarian.
- **Best-fit tracks:** Health, Sustainability & Society or Applied AI & Automation, depending on the solution.

### Closing the Gap in Women’s Health

- **Sponsor:** Tech Exploration Lab Digital Health Challenge
- **Awards:** First place $500; second place $250
- **Prompt:** Address an unmet need, persistent gap, or overlooked experience in women’s health, where underfunding and limited research contribute to slower diagnoses and limited access to care.
- **Best-fit track:** Health, Sustainability & Society.

### Badgers Building for Badgers

- **Sponsor:** DoIT Academic Technology
- **Awards:** First place $500; second place $250
- **Prompt:** Create technology that makes learning, work, or campus life easier for a fellow Badger. Standout projects may be invited to keep building with DoIT.
- **Best-fit track:** Applied AI & Automation.

### Databricks Real-World Workflows Challenge

- **Sponsor:** Xorbix Technologies
- **Awards:** First place $1,000; second place $500
- **Prompt:** Build an agentic system on Databricks for one of two industry problems Xorbix serves. Full problem details are to be revealed at the opening ceremony, followed by a Saturday workshop. No Databricks experience is required.
- **Best-fit track:** Applied AI & Automation.

### Open Venture

- **Sponsor:** Strato VC
- **Awards:** First place $500; second place $250
- **Prompt:** Present the venture potential of the project already being built. Judging looks for market signal, founder-market fit, differentiation, and a path beyond the weekend.
- **Best-fit tracks:** All tracks, with particular alignment to Emerging Technology & Entrepreneurship.

### The Art of the Break (Agentic Stress Test)

- **Sponsor:** Tech Exploration Lab
- **Award:** First place $500 and the winning team named in a Tech Exploration Lab industry report. No second-place award was listed in the supplied brief. Every team that contributes a Break Card is credited in the report.
- **Prompt:** Stress-test your own AI agent. Submit a one-page Break Card describing a failure specific to how the agent was built, how often it happened, attempted mitigations, and what the team learned.
- **Best-fit tracks:** All tracks; any team with an agent may opt in.

## Judging and submission

- A project must meaningfully address each challenge entered. Simply using a sponsor's tool or API is insufficient.
- Submit the repository, a two-minute video, and required project responses by **11 AM Sunday**.
- Preliminary screening selects up to five finalists per track. Finalists demonstrate live **1–3 PM Sunday**; judges select first and second place in each track.
- Challenge winners are announced **September 30, 2026**. Teams do not need to be present to win a challenge award.
- A team may win its track and up to two challenges, for a maximum of **three awards**.

The supplied brief does not state the deadline timezone or a separate upload deadline for the Break Card. Check the submission form for those details. See [BUILDFEST-STRATEGY.md](BUILDFEST-STRATEGY.md) for the team's current entry plan.
