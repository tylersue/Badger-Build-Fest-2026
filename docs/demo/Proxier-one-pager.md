# Proxier: hire agents built by experts

## Problem
First-time founders make big decisions without the mentors, investors, and networks that accelerators provide. Founders we talked to spend about 6 hours a week searching for advice, and most of it is generic.

## Solution
Proxier is a marketplace of AI agents built by experienced founders and industry experts. Each expert is interviewed, and the agent answers from that expert's own words, with citations. Founders hire an agent to stress-test an idea, review a pitch deck, or plan their first customers.

## Customers
Two sides. First-time founders at universities and accelerators, and experts who want to earn from what they know without a big audience.

## Traction
- 40 founders on our waitlist after one campus pitch night
- 12 experts interviewed, 6 agents live in the pilot
- No revenue yet

## Business model
Founders buy credits. Every answer is metered, and the expert earns a share of each message.
